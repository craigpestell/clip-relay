# Positioning (draft)

Owner: `growth-lead`. Draft 2026-11-10. Assumptions to test are marked.

- **For:** people with a Windows, Linux or ChromeOS computer and a phone from a different ecosystem, especially iPhone. Apple and Microsoft already serve the same-ecosystem cases.
- **Problem:** getting a link, address, code or snippet from the computer to the phone today means emailing or messaging yourself.
- **Pitch:** "Select text, send it, tap Copy on your phone."
- **Why us:** works across ecosystems (Chrome extension → iPhone or Android), needs no shared Wi-Fi, end-to-end encrypted, and does one job with no setup beyond pairing.
- **Hook:** Chrome removed its own "send text to your devices" feature, and its users are a ready audience. (Assumption: they still look for a replacement. Test with search volume and forum threads.)
- **Channels to test:** Chrome Web Store search, r/iphone, r/chrome and r/productivity threads asking for this, and a Product Hunt launch. No outreach without the founder's approval.
